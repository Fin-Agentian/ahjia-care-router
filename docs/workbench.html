Actions A0–A5

          

          
A1, A3, A4 and A5 are the safety path: fixed rules, first on the NPU. A2 (the LLM) runs only when it is safe.

        

        
Three safety rules

          
    
            
  1. Instant call. “Help” or “can’t get up” (救命, 起不來) calls family at once (A5). Any voice counts, even the TV.
    2. 
            
  2. 30 s rule. For any other alarm, if nobody answers in 30 s, AhJia calls (A5). Only the elder’s own voice stops the clock.
    3. 
            
  3. Only two ways to clear. The elder says “I’m OK” and stands … s, or family confirms in the app. No AI can clear or lower an alert.
    4.
